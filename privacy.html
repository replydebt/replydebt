Privacy & Data Protection Notice

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Applicable frameworks: EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Articles 13 & 15-22) and Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Articles 10 & 11).

1. Publisher & Scope

Network Audit (https://networkaudit.github.io/) is an independent, MIT-licensed open-source browser utility published by Nayri Akbuğa (github.com/networkaudit/networkaudit.github.io). Network Audit is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LinkedIn Corporation.

2. Zero-Backend Client-Side Architecture

Network Audit has no application server, no backend API, no user database, and no account registration. When you select or drop a LinkedIn data export archive (.zip or .csv), the file is parsed exclusively inside your own web browser on your own device using local JavaScript and a same-origin Web Worker.

Every page enforces a strict Content Security Policy (connect-src 'none', form-action 'none') that instructs your browser to block outbound network transmissions of archive data. The publisher never receives, views, stores, or has technical access to your LinkedIn archive or any contacts inside it.

3. Archive Files Read Locally & Quarantined Sensitive Files

4. Browser Storage Modes, Minimization & 90-Day Expiry

Before opening any archive file, Network Audit presents a pre-open transparency prompt where local browser storage is turned OFF by default:

Mode / Storage Key What Is Stored on Your Device Retention
Memory-Only Mode (Default) Zero archive data is written to IndexedDB or localStorage. Results exist only in open-tab RAM. Cleared immediately when you close or reload the tab
Opt-In Saved Lists
(IndexedDB: network-audit)		 Minimized contact metadata (names, companies, titles, profile URLs, conversation IDs, dates, aggregate counts) and your done/dismissed/hidden marks. Raw message bodies, InMail subjects, invitation notes, email addresses, and the raw .zip file are never written to disk even when opted in. Auto-expires and purges after 90 days, or immediately via "Delete data from this browser"
UI Preferences
(localStorage: na.theme, na.handoff, na.notify)		 Color theme (light/dark), timestamp when you clicked "Open LinkedIn data download", and local notification toggle. Contains zero archive data. Until cleared via "Delete data from this browser" or browser settings

Shared computers & browser extensions: Do not open archives or enable local browser saving on a public or shared computer. Because third-party browser extensions granted "read and change all your data on all websites" permission can inspect page DOM content in any tab, use a Private / Incognito window (where extensions are disabled by default) when opening personal archives.

Third-party contact data: Your LinkedIn archive contains names, titles, and messages of your connections and correspondents. Use the locally generated lists solely for your own personal communication and household/professional follow-ups.

5. Static Web Hosting & Visit Analytics

  • Static Hosting (GitHub Pages): When your browser requests the static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files of networkaudit.github.io, GitHub, Inc. processes standard HTTP transport metadata (such as IP address and User-Agent) in server logs for content delivery and DDoS/security protection under the GitHub Privacy Statement (legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) / KVKK Art. 5(2)(f) legitimate interest in secure static file delivery). No archive file or parsed personal data is ever transmitted to GitHub.
  • Visit Analytics: Disabled at launch (ANALYTICS_ENDPOINT = ''). Network Audit sets zero cookies, loads zero third-party scripts, and sends zero analytics beacons.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR (Arts. 15-22) & KVKK (Art. 11)

Because all archive processing occurs exclusively on your own device and the publisher holds no personal data from your archive:

  • Access, Rectification & Portability: You hold the original LinkedIn .zip archive directly on your device.
  • Immediate Erasure: Close the browser tab (in default Memory-Only mode) or click Me -> Delete data from this browser (or clear site data in your browser settings) to permanently delete all locally saved lists and preferences.
  • Questions & Contact: For privacy or security inquiries regarding this open-source software, contact the publisher via GitHub Issues or report security vulnerabilities privately via GitHub Security Advisories. EEA and Turkish users also retain the right to contact their local supervisory authority (including the KVKK Board in Türkiye or their national EU Data Protection Authority).
